Jorel

Jorel is a personal assistant with a single user, its owner. It runs on the owner's own server and talks to the owner through a private Telegram bot. It is not offered to anyone else.

It keeps the owner's documents, notes, to-dos and reminders. With the owner's consent it reads the owner's Gmail, read-only: it finds the emails the owner asks about, and tells the owner when an email they are waiting for arrives. It cannot send, delete or change mail.
