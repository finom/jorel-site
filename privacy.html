Jorel privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026.

Jorel is a personal assistant with a single user, its owner. This policy covers the data it gets from Google.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, Jorel reads the owner's Gmail through Google's read-only scope (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or change mail.

How it uses the data

  • It searches the mailbox and reads the emails and attachments the owner asks about.
  • Every few minutes it checks for emails the owner said they are waiting for, and tells the owner when one arrives.
  • When the owner asks about an email, its text goes to the AI models that write the answer: Anthropic's Claude, and Google's speech model for a spoken answer, both through Vercel AI Gateway with zero data retention. They don't keep the text or train on it.
  • The answers reach the owner as Telegram messages.

What it stores

Only on the owner's server: the owner's watch rules, the IDs of emails it has already reported, the chat history with the owner, and the attachments the owner asks it to file.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used for ads and not used to train AI models. Jorel's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Jorel's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The data on the server is the owner's to delete.
